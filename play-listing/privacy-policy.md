# SplitSnap privacy policy draft

**Effective date:** Pending owner approval and publication URL.

SplitSnap is a local-only bill-splitting app. It does not require an account and does not operate a backend service.

## Data handling

- Bill amounts, participant names, item assignments, and settings are stored only on the device when the user saves or continues a bill.
- SplitSnap does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or use this data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or tracking.
- SplitSnap does not include ads, analytics SDKs, third-party tracking SDKs, payment processing, or account authentication.
- When the user explicitly copies or invokes the operating system share sheet, the selected plain-text result leaves the app under the user’s direction. Recipient apps handle that text under their own policies.

## Retention and deletion

Users can remove saved bills and reset local data from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes its app storage subject to the operating system’s normal backup and restore behavior; Android backup is disabled for the packaged app.

## Contact

A verified developer contact and public privacy-policy URL must be supplied by the owner before any Play submission.
