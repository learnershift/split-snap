## Full description

SplitSnap helps 2–8 people settle one bill fairly. Enter the subtotal, tax, tip, and participants; use equal, weighted, or itemized shares; then copy a plain-text summary. Exact rounding ensures every smallest monetary unit reconciles to the bill total.

SplitSnap is designed for the table: it is local-only, has no account, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no payment initiation. Your bill stays on your device unless you choose to copy or share the text yourself. The core flow works offline after the app is installed.
